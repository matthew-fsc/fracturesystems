Service lines

What we do

Five lines. An engagement uses one of them, or moves from assessment into a build and then a retainer. Scope and fee are written down before the work starts.

01

Architecture assessment

Fixed fee · 1–2 weeks

A systems inventory, a target architecture, and a roadmap. What is running, what it should become, and the sequence between the two. The written result is the thing you keep, whether or not you hire us to build it.

02

Platform implementation

Scoped to the architecture

CRM and custodian migrations, data platforms, integration engineering, APIs, and workflow automation. The build that follows an assessment, or a defined piece of one.

03

M&A and roll-up integration

One operating model across every acquisition

For private equity platforms, family offices, private wealth groups and other aggregators buying relationship-driven services firms. The same numbers from the platform close through each add-on.

  • $10,000 to $15,000 Systems and data diligence, 2 weeks, credited at close
  • $30,000 fixed Operating visibility, 6 weeks, read-only
  • From $10,000 Fold-in, per add-on, 3 weeks
  • $2,500 / month Hosting, support, and the monthly pack
The full offer, scope and terms
04

Intelligent automation

Guard-railed, inside regulated workflows

AI drafts, extracts and summarises. It never computes a number with financial consequence. Every figure traces to the source records.

05

Retainer

After an assessment or a build

The architect who did the work stays on for support and the changes that follow. Scoped once the systems are known. On a roll-up, support is the monthly pack, priced on that offer.
